# Welcome Email Template

Write a professional welcome email for a new customer.

**Customer Name:** {{customer_name}}
**Product:** {{product_name}}
**Plan:** {{plan}}

The email should:
- Welcome the customer warmly
- Confirm their subscription to the {{plan}} plan
- Highlight 3 key features of {{product_name}}
- Provide next steps for getting started
- Include a call-to-action
- Be friendly and professional

Keep the email concise (under 200 words) and scannable with clear sections.
